Two ways

Hot, and cold.

Hot

Thick, savoury, short

70°C / 158°F · 4 g · 200 ml · 2 min

Pour the cup out completely, to the last drop. Closer to a broth than to anything green.

Cold — Mizudashi

Sweet, thin, very cold

10 g · 1 litre of cold water · overnight

Into the refrigerator for four hours, or overnight. Cold water takes the sweetness and leaves the bitterness in the leaf.

Ask Mr. Moriuchi about the cold method while you are here — he recommends it as readily as the hot one. Not one as the real method and the other as a shortcut. Both.
