Founder Story

Why I Started ARKLinks

Tomoya Iwamae — Founder & CEO

I've always been drawn to the world beyond Japan's borders. That curiosity led me to study English at Waseda University, and later into the world of high-stakes corporate sales — presenting to C-suite executives at Japan's largest listed companies, closing deals that demanded both precision and trust.

After work, I found myself drawn to bars. Not just for the drinks, but for the conversations. It was there, glass in hand, that I started paying closer attention to Japanese sake — a drink unlike anything else in the world, born from Japanese water, rice, and generations of craft.

Nearly 70% of Japan's sake breweries are operating at a loss — not because their sake isn't extraordinary, but because the people behind it are artisans, not salespeople.

They've dedicated their lives to perfecting what's in the bottle. Getting it in front of the world? That's a different skill entirely. That's where I come in. A background in English, three years of corporate sales experience, and a genuine obsession with sake.

I started ARKLinks because I believe the world deserves to taste what Japan has been quietly perfecting for centuries — and because someone needed to tell that story in a language the world can hear.
