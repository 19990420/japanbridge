Japan's finest things are never in shops.

They rest in the hands of the people who make them. Behind every remarkable sake, every bowl of matcha, every grain of heirloom rice, there is a family — one that has refined a single craft for centuries. The deepest way to know Japan is to sit with these makers, walk their fields, and taste their work where it is born. That is what we arrange.

The Founder

Why ARKLinks exists

Travelling through Japan's countryside, I kept meeting extraordinary people: a tea farmer whose family has worked the same hillside since the Edo period, brewers and winemakers producing world-class craft in near anonymity. The travellers who found them were moved to tears — yet the world has almost no way to reach them.

At the same time, these makers — the very foundation of Japanese culture — are quietly disappearing. Successors are scarce, and centuries of craft risk ending in a single generation. I founded ARKLinks on one conviction: that genuine encounters between the world's most curious travellers and Japan's finest makers can carry this heritage forward — not through charity, but through lasting relationships.

My work is simple: I open the doors, translate the stories, and let Japan speak for itself.

Tomoya Iwamae — Founder
Notes for Guests

Quiet answers

How is a journey priced?

+

Each journey is composed once and quoted privately, reflecting the makers involved, the season, and the length of your day. Share your interests and we will respond with a proposal.

Do I need to speak Japanese?

+

Not at all. A licensed bilingual guide-interpreter accompanies every journey, and all correspondence is in English.

May we combine several crafts?

+

Most guests do. A tea farm in the morning, a vineyard in the afternoon — or several days across sake country. Tell us what moves you, and we will compose around it.

Can what we taste follow us home?

+

Yes. We arrange purchases and international shipping directly from the makers, and can continue seasonal deliveries long after you return.

How far ahead should we write?

+

Four weeks or more is ideal — harvests, brewing periods and picking seasons shape what is possible. Shorter notice is sometimes possible; please ask.

Do you work with hotels and travel designers?

+

Yes. We partner with hotel concierge teams and luxury travel designers to compose maker journeys for their guests. Please write to us for partnership details.
